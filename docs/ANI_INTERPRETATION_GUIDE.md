# ANI Interpretation Guide

Average Nucleotide Identity (ANI) estimates whole-genome nucleotide similarity between two genomes.

## Practical Thresholds

| ANI (%) | Common interpretation |
|---:|---|
| 99–100 | nearly identical strains or very close isolates |
| 96–99 | usually same species |
| 95–96 | common bacterial species boundary zone |
| 94–95 | borderline; inspect carefully |
| <94 | usually different species |

## Important Notes

ANI should not be interpreted alone. Always consider:

- genome completeness
- contamination
- assembly quality
- genome size
- taxonomy
- phylogeny
- ecological and phenotypic information
- gene content and pangenome context

## ANI vs dDDH

ANI and digital DNA-DNA hybridization are both used for genome-based species delimitation. ANI around 95–96% is often treated as broadly comparable to the historical 70% DDH species boundary, but exact correspondence may vary.
